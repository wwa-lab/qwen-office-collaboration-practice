## 1. Categories and evidence strength

- **A — directly supported fact:** original materials, tool calls/returns or attributed first-person statements reviewed here. Establishes what the material contains, not overall platform capability.
- **B — observed experience:** behavior from one or a few demonstrations, without systematic reliability testing. A records may support B observations.
- **C — analytical inference:** evidence-based explanations or management judgments, with conditions retained.
- **D — unverified hypothesis or proposal:** future uses, benefits, rollout and experiment design, not accomplished facts.

High/medium/low concerns evidence for **the specific row**, not statistical confidence or product ratings. “Unverified” means no results yet. These categories differ from M2's original A/B/C labels.

## 4. Four validation levels

| Level | Assessment | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Functional availability | Multiple calls and readbacks directly recorded. | One success does not establish reliability across all paths. |
| Scenario feasibility | Selected steps feasible with simulated data, one operator, preset skills/templates; contract entry also personally observed. | Missing write/confirmation context, differing tracking states and unverified delivery limit end-to-end acceptance. |
| Effectiveness validated | Not yet. | No comparable manual timing, common quality scores or handoff outcomes. |
| Scalable rollout feasible | Not yet. | Bank conditions, multi-person responsibilities, long-term stability and maintenance cost untested. |
